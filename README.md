# SmartKarobar

### Digital Khata & Business Management Platform for Nepal

SmartKarobar is a mobile-first digital ledger and business management platform designed for **shops, wholesalers, retailers, and small businesses in Nepal**.

It helps businesses move from traditional paper khata to a simple digital system for managing **customers, credit sales, payments, outstanding balances, vendors, statements, reminders, and business insights**.

---

## What is SmartKarobar?

SmartKarobar combines the simplicity of a traditional **khata book** with modern digital business tools.

Instead of maintaining separate notebooks for customers, payments, and outstanding amounts, businesses can manage their records from one place.

The platform is designed with the practical needs of Nepali businesses in mind, including **Bikram Sambat dates, customer groups, area-wise management, vendor tracking, and WhatsApp-based payment reminders**.

---

## Key Features

### Customer Ledger

Manage customer accounts and transactions from a single place.

* Credit sales tracking
* Payment recording
* Outstanding balance tracking
* Complete transaction history
* Customer statements
* Customer-wise transaction summaries
* Retail, wholesale, and VIP customer groups
* Area-wise customer organization

### Vendor Book

Track money owed to suppliers and vendors separately from customer receivables.

* Vendor transactions
* Purchase records
* Payments to vendors
* Outstanding payable amounts
* Vendor statements
* Complete vendor transaction history

### Payment & Collection Management

Keep track of daily collections and outstanding payments.

* Record customer payments
* Monitor unpaid balances
* Track payment history
* Identify overdue accounts
* Maintain collection records
* View customer-wise outstanding amounts

### WhatsApp Reminders

Make customer follow-ups easier with WhatsApp-based payment reminders.

Businesses can quickly communicate outstanding amounts to customers without manually preparing messages every time.

### Bikram Sambat Support

SmartKarobar is designed for Nepali businesses and supports **Bikram Sambat (BS) dates** alongside business transaction records.

### Customer Management

Organize customers according to business requirements.

* Retail customers
* Wholesale customers
* VIP customers
* Area/location groups
* Active customers
* Inactive customers
* Customer transaction history

### Reports & Analytics

Understand business records through organized reports and summaries.

* Total receivables
* Total payables
* Customer outstanding
* Vendor outstanding
* Payment history
* Transaction summaries
* Area-wise reports
* Customer activity

### AI-Powered Insights

SmartKarobar is designed to go beyond basic bookkeeping by using business data to provide practical insights.

AI-powered analysis can help identify:

* Payment patterns
* Customers requiring follow-up
* Potential collection risks
* Overdue accounts
* Expected payment behavior
* Important changes in customer activity
* Suggested follow-up actions

## Data Ownership

### Your Data Stays Yours

Business records are valuable, so SmartKarobar is designed with data portability in mind.

Users can export their business data for backup and future use.

Supported export formats include:

* CSV
* JSON

The goal is to make sure businesses are not unnecessarily locked into a single system.
